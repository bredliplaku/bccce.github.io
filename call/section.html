Paper Guidelines

We warmly welcome you to the official guidelines for conference submissions. We invite you to review the information below, which outlines our formatting practices and academic expectations to help you smoothly prepare your manuscript.

2. Guidelines

  • Works must be written in English.
  • Content must be original and not previously published or presented.
  • Provide accurate contact details for the primary author.
  • Indicate the relevant subtopic(s) for accurate peer-review allocation.
  • Name your file according to the convention prior to upload.

3. Submit to Portal

  • Submissions are exclusively processed via our secure online portal.
  • You will receive an account to track and modify your submission.
  • Submissions will be peer-reviewed and featured in our ISBN Book of Proceedings.
  • Publication requires successful presentation at the conference.

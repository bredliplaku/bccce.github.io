Why Sponsor? Why Exhibit?

National and International Audience

BCCCE attracts delegates from across Albania and also from more than 20 countries around the world. Here is your opportunity to see and be seen by a truly national audience, and to also present your company to a motivated international audience.

Educated and Experienced Attendees

BCCCE delegates are university educated. Many have undertaken post-graduate studies and possess post-graduate degrees. Delegates are experienced professionals, working in their respective fields for many years, many for their effective careers.

Designers, Specifiers and Decision Makers

BCCCE delegates are engineers who design and specify for a wide range of civil works in Albania and around Balkan countries. They also manage those who design, specify and purchase for a wide variety of projects, applications, clients and enterprises.

High Profile Targeted Promotion Opportunity

BCCCE is the premiere gathering of civil engineering profession in Albania, with delegates attending from around the world. Put your firm in front of those who make the decisions that can benefit your business. By sponsoring the 2027 BCCCE in Tirana, you will have exclusive access to this demographic. Also, by sponsoring our conference, you align yourself with this major event, showcasing your firm to our provincial, national and international delegates.
